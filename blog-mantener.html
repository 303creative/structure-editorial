How to keep your cut perfect between visits

Structure Men's Studio · West Brickell, Miami

A good cut doesn't end in the chair: it's kept up at home. These are the three habits that make your cut look fresh for longer — without the hassle.

1. Wash less, hydrate more

Washing your hair every day with harsh shampoos dries it out and the cut loses shape. 2–3 times a week is enough; the rest, just water or conditioner. A healthy scalp = hair that styles itself.

2. Right product, right amount

The #1 mistake is using too much. For natural finishes, a pea-sized amount of water-based pomade or clay. Apply to nearly dry hair, back to front. Less product, more control.

3. The 2-minute reset

In the morning, wet your hands, run them through the shape and add a touch of product. No need to wash again. That reset keeps the cut's line between visits.

How often should you come back?

A fade looks sharp ~2–3 weeks; a classic or scissor cut holds 4–6. At your appointment we'll tell you the ideal interval for your cut and lifestyle.
