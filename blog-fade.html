Skin fade in Miami: types and who they suit

Structure Men's Studio · West Brickell, Miami

The skin fade is one of the most requested cuts in Miami — and also one of the easiest to ruin when done wrong. Here we explain the types and which one suits you.

What is a skin fade?

It's a fade where the hair goes from longer on top down to skin on the sides and back, with no visible line. The key is the clean transition: done well, it looks sharp; done badly, you see "steps".

The types by height

Low fade: the fade starts low, near the ear. Subtle and elegant — works with everything and in formal settings.

Mid fade: starts at mid height. The most versatile and popular; balances contrast without being aggressive.

High fade: starts high, maximum contrast. Modern and bold — ideal if you want it to stand out.

Taper: a softer fade, only at the sideburns and nape. The most conservative option, perfect for the office.

Who does each one suit?

It depends on your face shape and lifestyle, not on trends. A round face gains with more volume on top and a higher fade; an oblong one balances with a low or mid. That's why at Structure the fade starts with a consultation: we tell you which one flatters you before touching the clipper.

Maintenance

A skin fade looks flawless for the first 2 weeks and starts growing out fast at the edges. To keep it sharp, the ideal is to touch it up every 2 to 3 weeks.
