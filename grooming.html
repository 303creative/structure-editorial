THE GUIDE

Which finish are you after?

Matte

Natural, dry look with no shine — reach for a clay or a texture cream.

Shine

Slick, polished finish — a water-soluble pomade rinses out clean at night.

Wash

Match the shampoo to your hair: biotin for strength, argan for softness, rosemary mint to clarify build-up.

Beard

A balm softens coarse hair and helps shape the line between cuts.
