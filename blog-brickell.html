Barbershop in West Brickell: what to look for (and what to avoid)

Structure Men's Studio · West Brickell, Miami

Brickell is full of barbershops. Choosing well saves you weeks of 'corrections'. Here's what actually matters.

Look for: consultation before cutting

A good barbershop asks about your face, your routine and what you want it for — not just 'what number'. If no one advises you, it's a haircut factory, not a studio.

Look for: by appointment only

An appointment means they give you time, with no line rushing you. At a premium barbershop, quality isn't improvised against the clock.

Avoid: the photo cut with no judgment

Showing a photo is fine, but a good barber tells you whether that cut suits you or not. Saying 'yes to everything' ends in a cut that was never right for your face.

Avoid: unclear pricing

Clear services and prices = respect for your time. At Structure: Classic $50, Skin Fade $55, All-Scissors $60, and image consultation $75.
