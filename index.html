FAQ

Before
you book

Do you specialize in scissor cuts?

Yes. Our All-Scissor Cut is 100% scissor work, and most guided cuts use scissor work on top for natural texture and movement. Bri leads our scissor and guided cuts.

Where are you located and how do I find you?

1110 SW 2nd Ave, Suite 4, in West Brickell, Miami, FL 33130 — a private studio near Brickell. If you can't find Suite 4 when you arrive, message us on WhatsApp and we'll guide you in.

How much is a haircut?

Classic Cut $50, Skin Fade $55, All-Scissor Cut $60, Guided Cut $75, and the Structure Experience $115. Exact options are confirmed at booking on Squire.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. We work by appointment only so every client gets unhurried time in the chair. Book on Squire or ask Sofía to help you choose.

Which service should I choose?

If you're not sure, book the Guided Cut — we read your hair and face and recommend the right shape and technique. You can also ask Sofía before you book.
