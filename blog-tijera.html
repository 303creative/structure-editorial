Technique

Why scissor work changes the result

Structure Men's Studio · West Brickell, Miami

The clipper is fast; the scissors are control. When you want texture, movement and a finish that looks natural, scissor work makes the difference between 'a haircut' and your best version.

Clipper vs. scissors

The clipper evens out and fades fast — perfect for the sides and the fade. But for the top, where the style lives, scissors let you graduate the weight, create flow and respect how your hair grows. That's why an all-scissors cut looks softer and less 'blocky'.

Why it's slower (on purpose)

Cutting with scissors takes more time because every strand is measured and considered. That time is precision, not delay. The result lasts better and grows out more evenly.

The Structure method

We don't cut from a photo: we read your face and hair, define the style and execute it with the technique that style needs — often, scissors. That judgment is what our clients come back for.
