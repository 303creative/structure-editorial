Two-block haircut in Miami: what to ask your barber

Structure Men's Studio · West Brickell, Miami

A two-block can look sharp or shapeless — the difference is in the consultation.

What a two-block actually is

A two-block keeps length and texture on top while the sides and back are cut shorter and disconnected. Done well, it frames the face and reads clean in a professional setting. Done wrong, the top looks heavy and the disconnection looks accidental.

Who it suits

It works best on straight, dense hair with enough weight on top to hold shape — exactly the hair Alex works with every day at Structure. We adapt it to your growth pattern and your workplace, so it's modern without being costume-y.

What to ask your barber

  • Will you scissor the top so it keeps movement?
  • How short will the sides go — soft taper or hard disconnection?
  • How will it grow out over three to four weeks?
  • What should I do at home to style it?

If a barber can't answer those, keep looking.

No perm required

You don't need a perm to get a Korean-inspired shape. Alex builds it with scissor work and the right length, adapted to straight hair.
