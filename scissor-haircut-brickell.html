What an all-scissor cut is

A haircut done entirely with scissors. The clipper is fast and great for tight sides and fades, but the scissors give control: they let us build texture, blend length gradually and respect the way your hair actually grows. The result reads as intentional rather than uniform.

Who it's for

Men who wear a bit of length on top, who want movement and a natural finish, or whose hair goes blocky with clippers. It works across hair types — and for thick, dense or straight hair, Alex adds soft-taper techniques to control bulk and keep the shape for weeks.

Scissors or a fade?

Neither is "better" — they do different jobs, and many cuts use both. Scissors shape the top; a fade cleans the sides. In the chair we'll tell you honestly what suits your hair and how much upkeep each option needs. That judgment is the point of coming here.

How we do it

Every cut starts with a short read of your hair — density, growth pattern and how it falls when you leave it alone. We agree on a shape and the length it will hold in about four weeks, then execute it section by section. Before you leave, we show you how to keep it at home.
